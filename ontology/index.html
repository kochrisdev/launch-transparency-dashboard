LAUNCH Dashboard Ontology

Version 1.0.0 · namespace https://kochrisdev.github.io/launch-transparency-dashboard/ontology/launch.ttl#

Domain ontology for the LAUNCH (Launch Transparency Initiative) dashboard tracking new antimalarial medicines from regulatory approval to in-country access. Covers products, the 8-stage access pathway, per-stage status, milestones, country access levels, procurement volumes and the provenance/governance fields every value carries.

Controlled vocabularies · 8 schemes

Access pathway stages launch:PathwayStageScheme

The 8 ordered gates of a malaria product's journey from R&D to in-country delivery — three different kinds of approval (SRA regulatory, WHO guideline recommendation, WHO prequalification) plus registration, policy adoption and the delivery chain. Recommendation ≠ prequalification ≠ registration: three independent approvals run by different institutions. Concepts are generated from the data file's stages[] array with their positional index.

Concepts are generated into launch-data.jsonld from the data file itself, so they can never drift from the contract.

LAUNCH glossary launch:GlossaryScheme

Domain terms and their definitions as shown in dashboard tooltips. Concepts are generated from the data file's glossary object.

Concepts are generated into launch-data.jsonld from the data file itself, so they can never drift from the contract.

Stage status launch:StageStatusScheme

Traffic-light status of a product at a gate. Ranks match the flat projection: idle 0, late 1, prog 2, done 3.

notationlabeldefinition
idle Not started rank 0
late Delayed rank 1 The red light. Governance: must carry a substantive note, and the product must carry a top-level bottleneck flag — 'delayed' is an accusation in this domain and is never shown without a written reason.
prog In progress rank 2
done Complete rank 3

Country access level launch:CountryAccessLevelScheme

Ordinal access level of a product in a country; the highest applicable level wins on the map.

notationlabeldefinition
registered Registered rank 1 The national regulatory authority has licensed the product.
guidelines In national guidelines rank 2 Written into the national treatment guidelines — what health workers actually prescribe and the public sector buys.
mft In MFT strategy rank 3 Included in the country's multiple first-line therapies deployment.

Product class launch:ProductClassScheme

The two halves of the LAUNCH portfolio design: pipeline products stress the regulatory half of the pathway, marketed-but-underutilized products stress the adoption half.

notationlabeldefinition
pipeline Pipeline Pre-market — working through trials and approvals.
market Marketed Fully approved but underutilized — the adoption story.

Query it

The accountability query — which products are delayed, at which gate, why, and which institution owns that gate:

PREFIX launch: <https://kochrisdev.github.io/launch-transparency-dashboard/ontology/launch.ttl#>
PREFIX schema: <https://schema.org/>
PREFIX skos:   <http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#>
SELECT ?product ?stage ?reason ?owner WHERE {
  ?p launch:hasStageEntry ?e ; schema:name ?product .
  ?e launch:hasStatus launch:status-late ;
     launch:atStage ?st ;
     launch:note ?reason .
  ?st skos:prefLabel ?stage .
  OPTIONAL { ?st launch:operatedBy/schema:name ?owner }
}
